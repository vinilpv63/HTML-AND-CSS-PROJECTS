Simple Pancakes

These easy pancakes are perfect for a quick and delicious breakfast.

Ingredients

  • 1 cup all-purpose flour
  • 2 tablespoons sugar
  • 1 teaspoon baking powder
  • 1 cup milk
  • 1 egg
  • 2 tablespoons melted butter

Instructions

  1. Mix the flour, sugar, and baking powder in a bowl.
  2. Add the milk, egg, and melted butter.
  3. Mix until the ingredients are combined.
  4. Heat a pan over medium heat.
  5. Pour some batter into the pan.
  6. Cook until bubbles form, then flip the pancake.
  7. Cook the other side until golden brown.
  8. Serve and enjoy!
